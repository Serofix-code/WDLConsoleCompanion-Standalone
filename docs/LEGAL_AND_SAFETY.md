# Legal, copyright, and safety rules

This unofficial community project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ubisoft.

Allowed repository material includes independently written code, factual offsets and hashes, short signatures needed for interoperability, independently written descriptions, schemas, and non-proprietary reproduction steps.

Do not commit game executables, DLLs, assets, source/decompilation dumps, save files, credentials, personal paths, account data, or material whose redistribution terms are unknown. Do not use the tools in multiplayer or anti-cheat-protected sessions.
