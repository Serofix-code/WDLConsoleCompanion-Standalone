# Camera research

Status: **UNKNOWN / UNDER DEVELOPMENT**.

Differential scans correlate writable private-memory floats with automated horizontal and vertical camera input. Earlier broad scans were slow and strict repeated filters reached zero candidates; this is evidence that the procedure or tolerance was unsuitable, not evidence that no camera transform exists.

Temporary structured CSV logs record phase, direction, candidate count, bytes scanned, elapsed time, and stage exports. Do not commit personal research logs. Once a candidate remains, it still requires adjacency analysis, cross-session reproduction, pointer stability, and a reversible read-only/nudge validation before any freecam write is implemented.

Remove or disable temporary CSV logging after the camera is identified and the minimal reproducible evidence is recorded here.
